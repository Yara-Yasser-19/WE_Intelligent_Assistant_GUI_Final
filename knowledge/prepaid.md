Checked: 2026-08-27

WE offers prepaid/control options including Super Kix and Tazbeet.

Super Kix displayed price points: 29, 37, 52, 69, 98, 120 and 150 EGP.
Tazbeet displayed price points: 46, 58, 105 and 165 EGP.

For 5G, Telecom Egypt states that customers with compatible devices can access 5G without an extra 5G service fee. The prepaid 5G page says activation can be done through *005# or the MyWE app, subject to offer terms.
