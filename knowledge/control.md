The WE Control portfolio includes WE MIX, Super Kix, Tazbeet and WE Club.

WE MIX examples:
- Mix 215: 3,500 MB mobile data, 1,500 minutes, 250 group intra minutes, 150 GB home internet and 50 SMS.
- Mix 310: 6,000 MB mobile data, 2,000 minutes, 500 group intra minutes, 190 GB home internet and 50 SMS.

Tazbeet examples:
- 46 EGP: 1,250 MB and 300 minutes.
- 58 EGP: 2,250 MB and 400 minutes.
- 105 EGP: 5,250 MB and 500 minutes.
- 165 EGP: 11,250 MB and 600 minutes.

Super Kix ranges from 29 EGP for 750 Kix up to 150 EGP for 6,500 Kix. The official page states that one Kix can be used as one minute to WE or other networks and internet usage consumes the bundle by MB.
