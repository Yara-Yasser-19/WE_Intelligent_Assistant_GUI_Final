# WE Gold
Source: https://www.te.eg/en/personal/mobile/we-gold
Checked: 2026-08-27

WE Gold is Telecom Egypt's premium postpaid mobile portfolio.

Current displayed price points include:
- WE Gold 300: 5.5 GB mobile internet and 1,200 minutes/SMS.
- WE Gold 600: 20 GB and 3,700 minutes/SMS.
- WE Gold 890: 30 GB and 6,000 minutes/SMS.
- WE Gold 1200: 65 GB and 8,000 minutes/SMS.
- WE Gold 1500: 70 GB and 9,000 minutes/SMS.
- WE Gold 2300: 130 GB and 10,000 minutes/SMS.

Higher tiers add benefits such as family sharing, home internet or WE Air options, roaming quota, international minutes and device-installment benefits. The official page states bundle prices are tax exclusive.
